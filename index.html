video-knowledge-pipeline

video-knowledge-pipeline is a personal tool. It reads the owner's own YouTube playlists and turns the videos in them into notes in a private, local notes collection.

The tool has one user: its owner. It is not offered to other people.

What the app does with Google data

  • The app asks for read-only access to YouTube (the youtube.readonly scope).
  • It reads the names of the owner's playlists and the videos in them.
  • It does not change, upload, delete or comment on anything.

Privacy policy

  • The app reads only the playlists of the signed-in owner.
  • All data stays on the owner's own computer. The app does not send it to any server, and it does not share, sell or publish it.
  • The app does not use the data for advertising or to train general AI models.
  • The sign-in token stays on the owner's computer. To remove the app's access, open Google Account permissions and remove video-knowledge-pipeline.

The app's use of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
